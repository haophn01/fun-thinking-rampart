Gaps and angles

  • Open-source CMMC trackers are thin: 1 star and 0 forks for the only self-hosted one.
  • Objective-level (320) tracking is thin: DefectDojo is per requirement, ControlMap names 171A without describing it, Drata displays objectives. (DefectDojo, ControlMap, Drata)
  • Scoring is inconsistent: Drata won't compute SPRS; DefectDojo calls its score advisory.
  • SOC 2-origin tools treat CMMC as a thin overlay and are heavy or costly for CMMC-only use (competitor blog). (ComplyJet)
  • Single-contractor framing is underserved: Apptega and ControlMap lean MSP.
  • SaaS vendors avoid CUI: Secureframe "cannot collect and store CUI". (source)
  • Catalog data: NIST OSCAL has only rev3; 171A is withdrawn and the PDF wins over CSV/XLSX. (NIST)
  • Regulatory churn: vendor-reported July 13, 2026 suspension. (Secureframe, Exostar)
  • User-review evidence is scarce; pricing is mostly hidden (only ARMS public).

Best practices

Score by the book

Start 110, subtract 5/3/1 per NOT MET. 42 five-point, 14 three-point, 54 one-point (arithmetic from the page). MFA 3.5.3 and encryption 3.13.11 are partial credit (-3 or -5). 32 CFR 170.24

POA&M as rules engine

Score at least 88; items 1 point or less (3.13.11 at 3 is the exception); six requirements barred; 180-day closeout. 32 CFR 170.21

Evidence per objective

Every objective carries evidence. Drata

Catalog separate from records

Mirror OSCAL: catalog, SSP, linked POA&M. Import/export, don't author in OSCAL. OSCAL SSP, POA&M, trestle

SSP is mandatory

CA.L2-3.12.4 needs an SSP at assessment; a POA&M doesn't replace implementing. Keep the POA&M living.

CUI hosting

External clouds holding covered defense information need FedRAMP Moderate equivalent; incidents reported in 72 hours. Self-hosted case not addressed in the clause fetched. DFARS 7012

Ideas to consider

  • Objectives as first-class rows; derive requirement status, SPRS and SSP from them.
  • Pure-function scoring and POA&M library with table-driven tests (plus known DoD example scores, own idea).
  • Conditional status view: live score, 88 line, barred-requirement warnings, 180-day countdown.
  • Versioned catalog file with checksum and per-family count test.
  • Rules and weights as data, not code (own idea).
  • Evidence store behind an interface with hashing and encryption at rest (local now, S3/Blob later).
  • Self-hosted single-org positioning that can hold CUI evidence.
  • OSCAL-compatible, not OSCAL-native, data model with OSCAL JSON plus DOCX/PDF export (own idea).
  • Fork or study clucraft (MIT) or CISO Assistant (check AGPL first).
