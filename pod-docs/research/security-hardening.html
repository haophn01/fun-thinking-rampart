Gaps

  • No peer publishes a mapping of its own controls to NIST 800-171. Drata and Eramba: no evidence found.
  • file-type does not detect zip/docx bombs. Use a size cap and a worker with a timeout (source).
  • Helmet is on /api only, so the served HTML has no CSP (source).
  • NIST gives no numeric password minimum on the page fetched. Secondary sources say 8 or 15 (source).

Best practices

  • OWASP Top 10 2025 order: Broken Access Control, Misconfiguration, Supply Chain, Crypto, Injection, Insecure Design, Auth Failures, Integrity, Logging, Exceptional Conditions (source).
  • Passwords: no composition rules, blocklist, long passwords allowed, rate limiting first (NIST).
  • Sessions: explicit Secure/HttpOnly/SameSite, __Host- prefix, rotate on login, server-side idle (15-30 min) and absolute (4-8 h) timeouts (OWASP).
  • pnpm: committed lockfile, allowBuilds, minimumReleaseAge (default 1 day), trustPolicy no-downgrade (pnpm).
  • Node: request timeouts, reverse proxy, guard prototype pollution, no --inspect (Node).
  • ASVS 5.0.0 (May 2025) as the checklist (source).
